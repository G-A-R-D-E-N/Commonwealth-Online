# Commonwealth Online 2.1.0

Commonwealth Online 2.1.0 makes playing together feel like a real game. You can keep several characters, talk to nearby players, revive a fallen friend, join a faction, capture settlements and see everyone on a live map. Hosting with Steam is easier, servers can require a mod pack, and a new Launcher sets everything up for you. A lot of connection problems and time-outs are fixed too.

## Highlights

- **Character slots.** Keep more than one character on your account.
- **Voice chat** with the players near you.
- **Downed and revive.** You are downed instead of dead, and a teammate can pick you up.
- **Factions** with ranks, a message of the day and their own chat.
- **Capture settlements** and control who builds in them.
- **A live map** with players, Far Harbor and Nuka-World, and fog of war.
- **Steam hosting and invites** straight from the pause menu.
- **Mod pack check** that tells you what to install and does it for you.
- **A full Launcher** for setup, play, mods, servers and logs.
- **Many connection and time-out fixes**, especially for players far from the server.

## Joining and playing together

- **Welcome flight.** The first time you leave Vault 111, the camera rises over the Commonwealth toward downtown Boston with a "Welcome to Commonwealth Online" banner.
- **Character slots.** Each account has several slots. A new window lets you pick a slot, create a character, name it or delete it, with a confirm step so you can't delete by accident. Picking a slot reconnects the game so your join finishes properly. Each slot keeps its own progress, friends and known names. Characters you made before slots are moved into your first slot. New characters use the normal Fallout 4 face editor.
- **New server browser.** It looks like Fallout 76's, with a multiplayer profile page. Each server shows its name, players and whether it answers. Servers with passwords are listed. Servers list themselves, and the official servers always stay on the list. The list shows right away and the "answering" check fills in a moment later.
- **Mod pack check.** A server can require certain mods, in a certain order, including extra plugins and BA2 archive files. If yours don't match, the game stops you and names the modlist you need. You can then agree to a download, the game installs it step by step and rejoins for you. Large mod lists arrive in pieces, and the download needs the server password if there is one. Different load orders on each player's PC are handled.
- **Host with Steam.** Press Start Hosting inside Commonwealth Online. It opens a Steam lobby that only your friends can see, friends show up as they connect, and Enter world takes everyone in together. You don't have to start a server yourself. Friends can join from a Steam invite, from the Steam friends list, or by double-clicking a friend in the Friends tab, which also shows which of your friends play Commonwealth Online. If Steam or its overlay is off, the game says so. The host is an admin automatically. After a few wrong passwords a player is locked out for a while, but never the host.
- **Invite Friends.** A host already in the world gets an Invite Friends button in the pause menu that opens the Steam invite window. If you are already in a game when an invite arrives, the game tells you why it was ignored.
- **Voice chat.** Talk to nearby players with push-to-talk (B by default) or an open mic. You hear people from where they stand. In settings you can mute or turn down any player, test your mic level and change the keys.
- **Chat.** Chat is on by default, has its own window, and has a separate faction channel. Game keys are paused while you type.
- **Friends.** A friends list that is saved between sessions. Type a name in the add-friend box and the game tells you the request is queued. Friends can see each other's names even if you haven't met in game, and a name stays known if you stop being friends. Your quest window shows friends' progress (hidden on solo quests). A quick-action wheel opens on another player, works on a controller, and pauses movement and look while it's open.
- **Strangers.** Players you haven't met show as "Stranger" until you know them. You always see your own name, your friends, your faction and admins.

## Map

- **Press M** to open the Commonwealth Online map. Offline, M still opens the Pip-Boy map.
- **See other players.** You, your fireteam, your faction, friends and everyone else look different. Enemies fighting you show as red dots. You can filter each group.
- **Three maps.** Tabs for the Commonwealth, Far Harbor and Nuka-World.
- **Fog of war.** The map is dark except around places you've found and where you stand.
- **Captures.** A settlement being captured pulses a red circle for everyone.

## Fighting, health and saving

- **Downed and revive.** When your health runs out you are downed instead of dead. A teammate can revive you, and the game tracks stimpak counts so a revive uses one properly. If nobody comes, you pick a respawn from a menu and wake at Sanctuary with your gear restored. If you disconnect while dead, you are brought back. Other players see your downed and dead state, and the roster shows everyone's health.
- **Other players look right.** You see the weapon they're holding, their armor mods and power armor. Their shots, attacks, health and deaths show up, and their running is smoother.
- **Your character is yours.** SPECIAL, XP, level and perks are saved and checked for each player instead of being shared.
- **Reconnecting.** If you drop, your spot is held for 60 seconds and you rejoin where you were.
- **Saving.** Normal and auto saves are turned off during online play. The game saves for you when you quit or disconnect, shows a notice, and tells you if a save was refused. Quitting from the pause menu no longer gets stuck. Your online profile follows the character, including saves made after you leave the vault, so rejoining picks up where you were.
- **Trading.** Trade items with another player in a trade window. Both sides swap at once.
- **Free roam.** Story and companion characters stay in the world but can't be talked to, and story quests are properly ended.
- **60 FPS limit.** Play is capped at 60 FPS. Loading screens and menus are not.

## Quests with a fireteam

- Form a private fireteam. Your quest progress isn't copied onto other people.
- Kills count for teammates who have a matching objective.
- Teammates on a shared story stay on the same step, and nobody is moved back.
- Switch a quest between solo and shared in the quest window. It shows teammates' progress.

## Settlements and factions

- **Capture a settlement.** Walk up to its workbench and press H. The screen shows the settlement's name, the progress and, if it fails, why.
- **Building.** The owner chooses who can build. Doors open and close for everyone, and scrapped objects stay scrapped. Settlements now also have upkeep, power, door locks, raids, defenses and settlers. New players are safe from raids for 72 hours.
- **Factions.** Create a faction, browse the list, invite players and leave. Leaders set a description, a colour and a message of the day (up to 1000 characters) that members see on the Members tab. Officers can invite and kick; leaders can promote, hand over leadership, or disband (if the leader is offline, an online member takes over). Invites show the faction tag and tell you when they expire or when the faction is gone. The faction window has four tabs and shows each online player's tag. Faction members are marked on the map and have their own chat channel. Faction names and tags with blocked words are refused. A server can optionally stop members of the same faction from hurting each other.
- **Bad language filter.** Slurs are always blocked. Swearing is blocked if the server turns it on, or if you press the "Bad words" button in chat. Blocked words are masked with stars, and names that contain them are refused.

## Menus

- **Pause menu.** The Server and Help buttons now work. Server shows the name, address, players and your ping. Help lists your controls.
- **Report.** Report a player for harassment, cheating or griefing, or ask an admin for help.
- **New look.** Chat, alerts, quests, the Steam lobby, the server browser and the downed screens have a new Pip-Boy style.

## For server owners

- **Admin menu in game.** Kick, ban, mute, change the time of day and manage factions from a menu with plain buttons. Admins can be added and removed.
- **Settings in game.** Change every server setting from the in-game menu. Changes apply right away.
- **Bans and kicks.** Your appeal link can be shown to banned players. Cheating attempts such as teleporting or huge damage earn strikes that lead to a ban.
- **Combat options.** Choose how VATS works (off, real time or normal) and whether players can hurt each other, or their own faction.
- **Raids.** Set raids to always, certain hours, or off.
- **Launcher.** A new desktop app. A setup wizard finds your game and tells OG, NG and AE apart. It works with Mod Organizer 2 and Vortex, helps you get F4SE with step-by-step Nexus instructions, and checks PrismaUI and the address database. PLAY starts the game through Mod Organizer 2, waits for Steam and reports any failure. MODS shows and switches your Commonwealth Online version, and warns if other mods block it. The SERVER page installs the bundled server as a mod, turns it on, and has Overview, Players, Admin, Settings and Raids tabs. A crash scanner and a Share Logs button make a zip with private details removed. The Launcher checks for new signed versions, updates itself, and can tell a Wabbajack install from a standalone one.
- **Scripts.** Servers can run their own scripts to add chat commands, react to events and show their own windows.
- **AFK kick.** Players idle for 15 minutes are kicked and told why. Before, they were kicked after 60 seconds. Loading screens don't count as idle.
- **Safer servers.** Your game now checks it's talking to the real server, and more limits stop abuse.

## Fixed

- **Connection problems and time-outs.** Players, EU players in particular, were being dropped or refused for no reason. The old 60-second idle kick judged activity wrongly, and the connect and welcome waits were only 3 seconds, which is too short over the internet. Both are fixed: the wait is now 10 seconds on the internet and the idle kick is a 15-minute AFK kick that says why.
- **Rejoining after a drop.** If you reconnect while the server still holds your old session, the game now says "Reconnecting..." and retries for you (up to about 20 seconds) instead of refusing you.
- **Lost last message.** When a server refused you and hung up at once, you only saw a generic disconnect. You now see the real reason.
- **Join gets stuck.** Cancelling a join now stops the download and the waiting. A failed connect or a game world reload no longer leaves the join screen stuck, and a stale cancel doesn't carry over to the next try.
- **Server browser.** A slow or broken server check can no longer freeze or crash the game. An old refresh can't overwrite a newer one. Servers over IPv6 and servers behind slow lookups are listed and checked correctly.
- **Steam.** Steam joins report why they failed, including time-outs and an older host that can't explain a refusal. Dropped Steam links are cleaned up and replaced without losing the new one. A Steam guest keeps the same profile each time they join.
- **Big joins.** The mod list and your starting items now arrive in small pieces and retry when the connection is busy, so a join no longer fails on a slow line.
- **Quit and save.** Quitting the game no longer hangs on a save, and a refused exit save now tells you.
- **Server list.** Servers register and un-register on the directory over the same connection type, so they no longer show as gone or stale.
- Typing a name in character select no longer loses focus, and Enter no longer opens chat there.
- Steam joins that time out now say so. Joining an older Steam host now says the host needs updating.
- The admin menu no longer shows raw code.
