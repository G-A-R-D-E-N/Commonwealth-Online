# Commonwealth Online 2.2.0

Commonwealth Online 2.2.0 adds more things to do together and fixes a lot of rough spots. You can sleep through the night with a shared sleep menu, build player walls that can be damaged, trade from the wheel menu and set up a server from your Mod Organizer 2 profile. Hunger, thirst and sleep now show as bars, and the menus work with a controller. The Launcher can now check your setup and tell you what is wrong. Saves are safer, the camp kit works properly, and the game runs smoother with two or more players.

## Highlights

- **Sleeping.** A sleep menu, a shared clock, and other players shown asleep.
- **Player walls take damage.** Melee and guns hurt them, and a health bar shows what is left.
- **Trade from the wheel.** Open the wheel on another player and pick Trade.
- **A new interaction wheel** with icons and a clear background.
- **Survival bars.** Hunger, thirst and sleep show above your health.
- **Controller support.** The Commonwealth Online menus work with a controller.
- **One DLL.** A single CommonwealthOnline.dll now works on Fallout 4 OG, NG and AE.
- **GOG.** Copies of Fallout 4 from GOG can join.
- **Launcher troubleshooter.** One button, CHECK MY SETUP, finds common problems.
- **Smoother play** with two or more players.
- **Safer saves and joins.**
- **PrismaUI 2.2.0.7** comes in the download.

## Playing together

- **Sleep.** Open the sleep menu to rest. All players share one sleep clock, and you can see other players asleep.
- **Player walls.** Hit a wall with melee or a gun and it takes damage. A health bar shows how much is left.
- **Trade.** The player wheel now has a Trade button. A trade finishes only after the server has saved it.
- **The wheel.** The player wheel is now a round menu split into pieces, each with an icon. The background is clear so you can see the world.
- **Survival.** Hunger, thirst and sleep show as bars above your health.
- **Controller.** The D-pad, Esc or B, the right stick, and the X and Y buttons work in the Commonwealth Online menus. You can open chat with a controller and type with the on-screen keyboard. Push-to-talk can be set to a controller button, and a second controller works too.
- **Multiplayer settings.** The 13 controls that did nothing now work and save. Esc or B asks whether to apply or discard your changes.
- **Menus.** Extra overlays are gone and the multiplayer panels have moved to better places.
- **Map names.** Player names show on the map dots, and a Names button hides them. Names of strangers hide when the map is zoomed out.
- **Map legend.** The legend lists your party and faction members. Click one to center the map on them.
- **Travel to players.** Pay caps to travel to a friend, party member or faction member from the map. The price depends on distance. You cannot travel in combat or without enough caps.
- **Settlement windows.** Search at a blocked workbench to open a card with Capture, Full menu and Decline. The settlement list has the Capture button again. The prompt shows the settlement's real name, and the faction window lists only settlements you have found.
- **Server list.** The server browser refreshes every 10 seconds while it is open, and shows live player counts.
- **Shared quests.** Sharing a quest needs a fireteam, and the quest window says so.
- **NPCs.** NPC health is shared between players, and NPC deaths reach everyone more reliably.
- **Seats.** Players sitting on a bench or chair now sit on other screens too. Power armor is left out.
- **Doors.** Door open and close changes sync both ways, and doors show their current state when you join.
- **Terminals.** Terminal locks now sync between players.
- **Pip-Boy light.** Other players' Pip-Boy lights show for everyone, in their Pip-Boy colour. In power armour the light sits at helmet height.
- **Power armor.** Other players' power armor keeps its frame and pieces. Paint and mod changes show without waiting for the next update.
- **Out of range.** Players who walk out of range disappear for you instead of freezing in place.
- **Voice mutes.** Mutes now follow the player, not the name. Old mutes carry over when the name is unique.
- **Faction chat.** Each faction keeps its own chat history, so a busy faction cannot push out another faction's messages.
- **GOG.** Copies from GOG can join. On a server that needs Steam, they are refused unless a Steam ticket checks out.
- **Gun hits.** A hit now follows the shot that made it more closely.
- **Camp kit.** The camp kit is a real Commonwealth Online item on a table in Vault 111, with a "Find your camp kit" objective. It uses the Commonwealth Online camp printer model with a matching collision box, and it cannot be dropped while online.

## For server owners

- **Mod Organizer 2.** The dedicated server can build its mod list from your Mod Organizer 2 profile.
- **Nexus and Vortex.** Players can now hand a mod pack over from Nexus and Vortex.
- **Host folder.** If a leftover server was blocking the settings link, the host server folder is now filled in for you.
- **Safer downloads.** Mod pack downloads and server heartbeat checks are stricter.
- **Survival setting.** The survival bars are on by default. Turn off `survivalNeeds` to hide them.
- **Region.** A `region` setting sorts your server first for players in that region. It does not apply to Steam P2P.
- **Steam sign-in.** A new `require_steam_identity` setting turns away players who have no verified Steam identity.
- **Bans.** Bans no longer block the loopback address, so a local test player cannot lock out the host.
- **Player cap.** Servers can hold up to 100 players. LAN discovery, the Launcher and the Steam host default all match.
- **Client version.** A server can set a required client version and check players against it.
- **Reports.** Admins get a notice for each new report, and a new `reports` command lists them. The newest 200 are kept until the server restarts.
- **Admin menu.** It now has plain rows and buttons on five tabs: Players, Bans and admins, World, Factions and Scripts. IP bans can be lifted with Unban. Making an offline player admin by typing a profile ID is gone. Use the online-player button or the console.
- **Bad language.** The `profanity_filter` setting turns on swearing filtering. The server needs its blocked-words file and will not start without one. The Launcher's server settings have a Block bad language switch.
- **Settlement zones.** Zones can have names in `workshop-zones.json`. A server that keeps its old file keeps the wrong cells for the two DLC settlements until the file is replaced.
- **Starting items.** Starting items are recorded before they are handed out, so a crash cannot lose or double them. If the outcome is unclear, an admin has to approve the grant before it is sent again.
- **Player keys.** Each server makes a `.player-key-secret` file on first start. Voice mutes depend on it, so they are per server.
- **Auto restart.** A local server that stops answering is restarted by the host app or the in-game Launcher, up to 5 times.
- **Logs.** Lost ranged hits are counted, and the first loss and every 50th are logged.

## Launcher

- **CHECK MY SETUP.** A new Troubleshoot page checks your setup and says what to fix.
- **Loose intro scripts.** The check also finds loose intro script files that can get in the way.
- **Troubleshoot buttons.** They also end leftover servers, clear hosting link files, repair server files and reset the server folder.
- **Stray servers.** The game ends a leftover server on port 7777, and the Launcher ends hosting servers when the game closes.
- **Folders.** Set your Fallout 4 folder, Mod Organizer 2 instance and Vortex staging folder in the Launcher settings. PLAY uses your chosen Mod Organizer 2 instance, and a button opens the Vortex staging folder.
- **GOG.** The Launcher finds Fallout 4 installed from GOG.
- **Server install.** Bundled server files up to 256 MB now install. The 90 MB Host.exe failed before.
- **One DLL.** One CommonwealthOnline.dll now works on Fallout 4 OG, NG and AE. Delete the old -NG and -OG DLLs and the UI DLL, or the mod refuses to load.
- **One download.** The release ships one client archive instead of three identical ones.

## Fixed

- **Camp kit.** Placing it, syncing it and camp failures were fixed. Failures that used to be silent now tell you. Your own camp is recognized after you reconnect, and its workbench only appears when you are in the camp's world.
- **Frame rate.** Drops with two or more players are fixed. They came from scanning NPCs and copying the voice list too often.
- **Saves.** Character saves are protected, and joining and menu recovery work better after a problem.
- **Character slots.** A new character no longer inherits another character's quest progress.
- **The popup.** "Saving is disabled in multiplayer" no longer shows.
- **Quests.** Reports from a lower quest stage are dropped quietly and finished quests are kept. Quests hidden by the world's rules are no longer saved as stopped. Clicking the scope you already have does nothing, and a solo quest shows Share quest instead of Go solo.
- **Settlements.** Defenders now keep their state, and object requests sent while you are still in the vault intro are no longer sent.
- **Objects.** Changed objects update when you walk into their range, not only when the cell changes.
- **Escape, map and captures.** Esc closes the admin and pause windows. The map lags less, and a stuck capture now says "Capture timed out".
- **First join.** First-time onboarding no longer disconnects you, and the welcome quest objective shows. The prompt tells you to use the server browser. The welcome flight camera no longer flips upside down.
- **Menus.** Menus take focus back after Alt-Tab, the selected server is easier to read, and the Steam buttons work. Chat and the Continue prompt no longer interrupt the SPECIAL confirmation. Fake menu data and demo alerts are gone.
- **Admin menu.** Rows no longer squish together, and the menu opens at the top.
- **Chat.** Typing keeps focus properly, and a message that cannot be sent now says so.
- **Settings.** Each setting says whether it applies at once or after a restart.
- **Out of Time.** Quest updates from other players no longer skip Codsworth's progress, and quest updates wait until the tutorial is ready.
- **Other players' looks.** Faces, hair and skin now appear on other players.
- **Other players' movement.** Remote players face the way they walk and strafe. A player whose updates stop for 10 seconds is moved to a holding spot instead of freezing where they were last seen.
- **Downed.** A downed player shows the downed pose instead of a T-pose, and late movement updates can no longer bring back an old downed state.
- **Shots.** After a respawn or a new slot, old shots no longer fire again on other players' screens.
- **Nearby NPCs.** NPCs from one area no longer vanish when another area sends an empty update.
- **Inventory.** The server now matches your real inventory. Starting items are sent again after each join until your game confirms them.
- **Names.** Nicknames and Steam names with hidden or right-to-left characters are refused.
- **Maps.** The pause map shows the Far Harbor and Nuka-World pictures, and dots and capture circles land in the right place.
- **Server list.** Official servers keep their region and OFFICIAL, CO-OP and USA badges.
- **Steam hosting.** More than 16 guests no longer fails. The limit is 17 players including the host.
- **Version mismatch.** The game tells you which version to update to.
- **Quest invites.** The invite list shows every player up to 100, not just 64.

## Under the hood

- PrismaUI 2.2.0.7 is now the version in the download.
- The build checks no longer build the game client on Linux, and the Windows checks start faster.
- Several test checks were updated to match the current safety rules.
- The UI API can report whether the Trade window is open and can open or close chat, for other mod tools.
- A debug-only build target for showcase footage stays out of the release.
