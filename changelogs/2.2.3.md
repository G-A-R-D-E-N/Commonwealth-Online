# Commonwealth Online 2.2.3

This update makes camps a bigger part of the game. You can move a camp, pack it up into a camp kit, or remove it. Settlements can now have repair and upkeep costs, raids, turrets, and locked doors. When you die, what you were carrying goes into a death bag you can go back for. Power armor and explosions are shared between players, trading has been rebuilt, and several freezes, crashes, and join problems have been addressed.

## Highlights

- **Death bags.** Items you were carrying when you die are kept in a bag where you died.
- **Move your camp.** Move a camp to a new spot, pack it up into a camp kit, or remove it.
- **Settlement costs and raids.** Repairs, upkeep, and recruiting settlers can cost materials. Owners can declare raids.
- **Settlement defenses.** Turrets fire at raiders, and owners can lock their doors.
- **Power armor.** Other players see the power armor you wear, and a parked suit stays where you left it.
- **Shared explosions.** Grenades, missiles, and Fat Man blasts can appear at the same spot for other players.
- **Vendors and trades.** A vendor window with a cart, and a rebuilt player trade window.
- **Pip-Boy in multiplayer.** Opening the Pip-Boy no longer pauses the game for you.
- **Stimpak revives.** Stimpak revives retry for longer before giving up.
- **Stability.** Fixes for freezes, reconnects, bad text, and NPCs that disappeared after a host change.
- **Launcher setup.** The launcher now walks new players through first setup and downloads Commonwealth Online for them.

## Launcher setup

- **Guided first setup.** The launcher walks new players through setup and downloads Commonwealth Online for them. The download is signed, and a bad signature, hash, or file size stops the install.
- **Game folder and mod manager.** The launcher helps you pick your game folder, then choose and install a mod manager.
- **Address Library.** The launcher can import the Address Library file you already have and link it for Mod Organizer 2, Vortex, or the game's Data folder.
- **Checks.** The launcher checks that F4SE is installed and scans your data files for problems.
- **Experimental mods.** The launcher asks you to acknowledge that playing with other mods is experimental.

## Playing together

- **Death bag.** When you die, the items you were carrying go into a death bag where you died. Your Pip-Boy and quest items stay with you. If the bag cannot be placed, a notice tells you whether your items were returned to you or are still in a container at your location.
- **Death bag withdrawals.** Take items out of the bag one at a time. Each withdrawal is saved on the server. The bag disappears once it is empty, and other players may still see it for up to about 10 seconds.
- **Death bag looting.** Only you can take from your bag unless the server allows other players to loot it.
- **Camp move.** Move your camp to a new spot. Moving it returns half of the materials its pieces cost. Only the owner can move a camp.
- **Pack up.** Sneak-activate the camp workbench to pack the camp up. The camp kit drops at your feet. You need to be in reach of the workbench.
- **Remove camp.** Only the owner can remove a camp. The move menu has a Delete camp option, and it asks you to confirm first. Removing an active camp refunds one camp kit. Removing a stored camp does not give a second kit.
- **Settlement costs.** Repairs, upkeep, and recruiting settlers can cost materials. The charge is recorded only after your inventory confirms the items are gone. If you pick up the same items again during the short confirmation window, the charge is refused.
- **Settlement repairs.** Owners can see a list of destroyed structures and rebuild them.
- **Capture screen.** The capture screen shows the workbench's health, raid protection, and your own protection, with a countdown and the reasons.
- **Raids.** Owners can declare a raid on a settlement. Declarations are saved, and the raid is shown again after you reconnect. Raid hits only count when you hold the kind of weapon the raid calls for.
- **Turrets and doors.** Turrets fire at hostile raiders but never at allowed builders. Settlement owners can lock their doors, and people without access cannot open a locked settlement door.
- **Settlement alerts.** Owners see a notice when their settlement is attacked.
- **Power armor.** Power armor you wear shows on other players, including when you enter and leave it. A remote player in power armor is shown standing, even if that player is sneaking. Stance is not synced yet.
- **Parked suits.** Leave a suit in a power armor frame and it stays there for other players to see. If you leave it while offline, the park is sent again when you reconnect. Only the owner can remove it, with the Remove suit button in the owner's menu.
- **Breaking into a parked suit.** Other players can try to break in with a hacking minigame. Breaking into a suit whose owner is offline is the hardest breach, and a wrong pick locks that player out of that suit for 30 seconds. The owner is warned when someone breaks in.
- **Parked suit menu.** The menu lists parked suits by distance, with place names and owner names.
- **Explosions.** Grenades, missiles, and the Fat Man are shared with other players, subject to the server's friendly fire setting. A blast you already see from the sender's own shot is not shown twice. Damage a shared blast applies to you is meant to be given back, so it should not hurt you; this is still being checked, so report any that do. Only vanilla explosions are shared.
- **Vendors.** Buy and sell with a vendor in a window with a cart. Buying costs the item's base value, and selling pays half. Vendor stock is kept in server memory, so it resets when the server restarts. A vendor sale that is not yet confirmed is also held in memory, so a server crash during one can lose those items.
- **Trading.** The player trade window is rebuilt with three columns: your offer, your partner's offer, and accept badges. It shows your partner's name. Looking at another player shows an interact prompt. A trade request that arrives while the pause menu is open waits until you close it, including Resume.
- **Pip-Boy.** In multiplayer the Pip-Boy no longer pauses the game for you. Solo play still pauses. If you go down with the Pip-Boy open, it closes.
- **Sleeping.** Wait, Pass Time, jail time and the vanilla sleep-wait menu are blocked in multiplayer.
- **Stimpak revive.** After a Stimpak is used on a downed player, the game keeps trying to stand them up for up to 8 seconds. If it still does not take, the player is brought back up where they are.
- **Save recovery.** After a server restart, your local save and the server's record are checked against each other. A recovery is applied only when your local save matches.
- **Survival needs.** Hunger, thirst, and sleep are kept by the server for each character. Eating and drinking are checked against your inventory, so an item cannot be used twice.
- **Reconnecting.** After a dropped connection, the reconnect window stays open through the retries, and your trade is kept when you resume.
- **Nearby players.** Other players nearby cost less to update each frame, which should help frame rate when several players are close together.
- **Map key.** Press M, Escape, or the controller B button to close it. M does nothing while another game menu or a Commonwealth Online window is open.
- **Multiplayer settings.** Online Status set to Nobody hides you from friend and faction lists and the player roster. Allow Friend Join lets Steam hosts accept friends always, ask first, or never. Ask First shows an alert, and a decline or no answer within 60 seconds refuses the join. Friend Requests can be Everyone, Friends of Friends, or Nobody. You can also turn on a ping and network stats overlay.

## For server owners

- **Death bag looting.** The `allow_death_bag_loot_by_others` setting controls whether other players can loot a death bag.
- **Settlements.** Non-owners building in an unowned named settlement is refused on the server. Up to four settlement charges that a player's game has not answered can wait at once; a fifth new charge is refused until one settles or is rejected.
- **Parked suits.** A parked suit whose owner has been offline for 14 days is removed. When the parked suit list is full, the oldest offline owner's suit makes room for an online owner.
- **Explosions and friendly fire.** Explosions are only shared when friendly fire is on. With faction friendly fire off, a blast is not shown to players in the sender's faction. With friendly fire off, NPC and object blasts are not shared either. Only explosions from the vanilla game are shared. Mod explosions are not.
- **Inbound limits.** Three new settings control how much data each player may send: `inbound_packets_per_second` (default 120, range 10 to 512), `inbound_bytes_per_second` (default 262144), and `inbound_violation_windows` (default 3). The first strike sends the player a private notice, and each strike is logged with the bytes used per message type.
- **Admin menu.** An admin who sends too many actions in a short time gets a "Too many admin actions" reply instead of silence. The limit is 16 actions in a burst, refilling at 4 per second.
- **Item spawner.** Admins can spawn items near a player, give or take items, and view a player's inventory from a new Spawner tab in the host app. Items are only sent to players whose client supports them.
- **Host server folder.** The in-game host server folder is found from the game's Data folder. The settings panel and the launcher use the same folder.
- **First-join recovery.** A first character that reached gameplay without a save can be discarded by an admin with the `first-join-abandon` command and a second, explicit confirmation of `discard-first-join-without-save`. The command refuses when that character is active, when the profile is connected, or when the character's starting grant names a character that has a recorded save.
- **Older clients.** The default required client version is now 2.1.0, so clients from before this release are refused when they join. Servers that set their own required version keep it.
- **Busy servers.** Saves only copy and check the data of the players who changed. Disconnects no longer wait for a save to finish, so a busy server should stall less while saving. If the server crashes in the few hundred milliseconds before that save lands, the player's latest state is lost.
- **Logs.** Dropped NPC hits now show up in release logs with the NPC's ID, and so do two kinds of dropped player-versus-player hits. LAN discovery logs say why a scan finds no servers, including requests from addresses that LAN discovery does not answer.
- **Death bag recovery.** A death bag still unplaced 24 hours after its placement request is archived for admin recovery. Admins can list and resolve archived bags with `death_bag_archive_list` and `death_bag_archive_resolve`.
- **Required difficulty.** Set the difficulty players must use with `required_difficulty`, in the settings file or the server settings panel. The levels are 0 Very Easy, 1 Easy, 2 Normal, 3 Hard, 4 Very Hard (the default, as before), 5 Survival, and 6 True Survival. A value outside 0 to 6 is rejected.
- **Enforce required difficulty.** This is the toggle that requires players to report their difficulty, now labelled this way. The required difficulty applies together with it. Servers and clients without the new setting behave as before.

## Fixed

- **Frozen players.** On older servers, a player who entered an interior could freeze for other players. This is fixed.
- **Remote health.** Another player's health is corrected if an earlier update did not apply.
- **Bad text.** A name, chat line, or other text with invalid characters no longer breaks the game's menus.
- **Main menu crash.** Commonwealth Online now waits 3 seconds after the main menu opens before its UI starts, to try to avoid a crash seen right after the menu opened.
- **First join loop.** A player whose first join was interrupted can now pick a character and join instead of looping on the slot selection.
- **Vanishing NPCs.** NPCs are less likely to disappear after a host change. Hidden NPCs are drawn again, and duplicates reported by two hosts are removed. If you still see invisible NPCs, report it.
- **Missing saves.** A join that fails because the character's save is missing now tells you why, instead of failing silently.
- **Inventory after reconnect.** Pickups, drops, and inventory updates from before a reconnect no longer apply to the new connection, so your inventory stays in step with the server.
- **Steam timeouts.** A Steam connection that times out now shows a readable "connection timed out" message.
- **Admin list.** The admin list and the Steam host port file are written safely, so a crash cannot leave the admin list cut short.
