# Commonwealth Online 2.2.3

This update makes camps a bigger part of the game. You can move a camp, pack it up into a camp kit, or remove it, and settlements can now have repair and upkeep costs, raids, and turrets. When you die, what you were carrying goes into a death bag you can go back for. Power armor, explosions, trading, and sleeping are shared between players, and several freezes, crashes, and join problems have been addressed.

## Highlights

- **Death bags.** Items you were carrying when you die are kept in a bag where you died.
- **Move your camp.** Move a camp to a new spot, pack it up into a camp kit, or remove it.
- **Settlement defenses.** Raids, door locks, and turrets that fire at raiders.
- **Power armor.** Other players see the power armor you wear, and a parked suit stays where you left it.
- **Shared explosions.** Grenades, missiles, and Fat Man blasts can appear at the same spot for other players.
- **Vendors and trades.** A vendor window with a cart, and a rebuilt player trade window.
- **Pip-Boy in multiplayer.** Opening the Pip-Boy no longer pauses the game for you.
- **Stimpak revives.** A Stimpak stands a downed player back up more reliably.

## Playing together

- **Death bag.** When you die, the items you were carrying go into a death bag where you died. Your Pip-Boy and quest items stay with you.
- **Death bag withdrawals.** Take items out of the bag one at a time. The bag disappears once it is empty.
- **Camp move.** Move your camp to a new spot. Moving it returns half of the materials its pieces cost.
- **Pack up.** Sneak-activate the camp workbench to pack the camp up. The camp kit drops at your feet.
- **Remove camp.** Only the owner can remove a camp. The move menu has a Delete camp option, and it asks you to confirm first. Removing an active camp refunds one camp kit. Removing a stored camp does not give a second kit.
- **Settlement costs.** Repairs, upkeep, and recruiting settlers can cost materials. The server only records the charge once your inventory confirms the items are gone.
- **Capture screen.** The capture screen shows the workbench's health, raid protection, and your own protection, with a countdown and the reasons.
- **Raids.** Raid hits only count when you hold the kind of weapon the raid calls for.
- **Turrets and doors.** Turrets fire at hostile raiders but not at allowed builders. Settlement owners can lock their doors.
- **Power armor.** Power armor you wear shows on other players, including when you enter and leave it. Other players in power armor do not show sneaking or crouching.
- **Parked suits.** Leave a suit in a power armor frame and it stays there for other players to see. Only the owner can remove it. Other players can try to break in with a hacking minigame. The owner is warned when someone breaks into it.
- **Explosions.** Grenades, missiles, and the Fat Man are shared with other players, subject to the server's friendly fire setting. Damage from a shared blast is given back to you, so it should not hurt you while you are still alive. Only vanilla explosions are shared.
- **Vendors.** Buy and sell with a vendor in a window with a cart. Buying costs the item's base value, and selling pays half. Vendor stock is kept in server memory, so it resets when the server restarts.
- **Trading.** The player trade window is rebuilt with three columns, your offer, your partner's offer, and accept badges, and it shows your partner's name. Looking at another player shows an interact prompt. A trade request that arrives while the pause menu is open waits until you close it.
- **Pip-Boy.** In multiplayer the Pip-Boy no longer pauses the game for you. Solo play still pauses. If you go down with the Pip-Boy open, it closes.
- **Sleeping.** Wait and Pass Time are blocked in multiplayer.
- **Stimpak revive.** After a Stimpak is used on a downed player, the game retries the stand-up for up to 8 seconds. If it still does not take, the player is brought back up where they are.
- **Reconnecting.** After a dropped connection, the reconnect window stays open through the retries, and your trade is kept when you resume.

## For server owners

- **Death bag looting.** A server setting controls whether other players can loot a death bag.
- **Settlements.** Building in an unowned named settlement is refused on the server.
- **Explosions and friendly fire.** Explosions are only shared when friendly fire is on. With faction friendly fire off, a blast is not shown to players in the sender's faction. With friendly fire off, NPC and object blasts are not shared either.
- **Admin menu.** An admin who sends too many actions in a short time gets a "Too many admin actions" reply instead of silence. The limit is higher than before.
- **Item spawner.** Admins can spawn items near a player, give or take items, and view a player's inventory from a new Spawner tab in the host app. Only players whose client supports it are affected.
- **First-join recovery.** A first character that reached gameplay without a save can be discarded with a second, explicit confirmation in the admin console.
- **Older clients.** The default required client version is raised, so clients from before this release are refused when they join. Servers that set their own required version keep it.
- **Busy servers.** Saves only copy and check the data of the players who changed, and disconnects no longer wait for a save to finish, so a busy server should stall less while saving.
- **Logs.** Dropped NPC hits now show up in release logs with the NPC's ID, and so do two kinds of dropped player-versus-player hits.

## Fixed

- **Frozen players.** On older servers, a player who entered an interior could freeze for other players. This is fixed.
- **Remote health.** Another player's health is corrected if an earlier update did not apply.
- **Bad text.** A name, chat line, or other text with invalid characters no longer breaks the game's menus.
- **Main menu crash.** Commonwealth Online now waits 3 seconds after the main menu opens before its UI starts, to try to avoid a crash seen right after the menu opened.
- **First join loop.** A player whose first join was interrupted can now pick a character and join instead of looping on the slot selection.
- **Vanishing NPCs.** NPCs are less likely to disappear after a host change, and duplicates reported by two hosts are removed. If you still see invisible NPCs, report it.
- **Admin list.** The admin list and the Steam host port file are written safely, so a crash cannot leave the admin list cut short.
