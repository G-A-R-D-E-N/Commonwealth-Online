# Commonwealth Online 2.2.0.1

This update makes it easier to get back on your feet, pick up your camp kit, and capture a settlement. Player names and faction tags are easier to see, survival bars are easier to read, and several problems with movement, menus, connections, and crashes have been fixed.

## Highlights

- **Get back up.** Use the button on the downed screen to revive yourself.
- **Help a teammate.** A prompt now appears when you can revive a downed player.
- **Camp kit.** The kit in Vault 111 can be picked up and placed.
- **Settlement capture.** Attacks on a settlement workbench now count toward breaking it.
- **Player names.** Nearby players have names above their characters, and faction tags appear in chat.
- **Clearer survival bars.** Hunger, thirst, and sleep are larger and easier to read.
- **Smoother play.** Other players' movement, joining, menus, and stability have improved.

## Playing together

- **Self-revive.** Use the button on the downed screen to get back up. The number of Stimpaks needed follows the server's settings.
- **Revive prompt.** When you are near a downed teammate, the prompt shows their name and how to help them.
- **Revive costs.** Helping a teammate uses the Stimpak cost set by the server, including when a revive is instant. A cancelled revive no longer charges the teammate.
- **Downed controls.** A downed player can no longer move, fight, reload, throw, or sneak while waiting to be revived.
- **Admin revives.** A player revived by an admin no longer remains stuck in the death camera or bleedout pose.
- **Camp kit.** The camp printer in Vault 111 can now be targeted, picked up, and placed.
- **Workbench damage.** Hits on a settlement workbench now count as damage, so it can be broken during a capture.
- **Capture prompts.** The faction capture window closes when a capture succeeds, and the notice shows the new owner.
- **Player names.** Names appear above nearby players, with their faction tags when available.
- **Faction tags in chat.** Chat messages show the sender's faction tag beside their name.
- **Survival bars.** The hunger, thirst, and sleep display is larger, scales with your screen, and stays easier to see.
- **Movement.** Other players are less likely to slide, half-walk, or stay crouched when they have stopped.
- **Player collisions.** Other players are less likely to block your movement unexpectedly.
- **Trade.** The interaction wheel closes when a trade starts, and the trade window appears above it.
- **Building.** When a workshop placement is refused, the game now tells you why.
- **Map.** Player markers are easier to see, and location names are clearer.
- **Controller vibration.** Changes to vibration settings refresh properly.
- **Teleporting.** The screen overlay is less likely to stay stuck after a teleport.
- **Unique characters.** Unique characters are matched more reliably across players' games.

## For server owners

- **Survival difficulty.** Survival servers can require Very Hard difficulty. Admins are exempt from the check.
- **Player reports.** Admins can see recent reports in the admin menu. Reports no longer disappear to make room for other server records.
- **Weather.** Weather chosen by an admin no longer changes back to the host's weather.
- **Admin travel.** Admins no longer pay caps to travel to another player from the map.
- **Admin movement.** God mode, noclip, and teleporting send fewer unnecessary updates to the server.

## Fixed

- **Joining.** Players are less likely to be disconnected during the burst of activity when joining, and the disconnect message explains what happened.
- **Automatic weapons.** Firing an automatic weapon is less likely to cause a disconnect.
- **Crashes.** Fixed a crash that could happen when changing areas near other players.
- **First join.** If a first-time join is interrupted before a save is made, the player can retry instead of being stuck.
- **Settlement building.** Build zones recognize plugin names regardless of letter case.
